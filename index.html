GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

A few useful things to know before your first enquiry.

How do I start a project? +

Fill out the enquiry form with a few details about your idea. We'll use that to understand the project and discuss the next steps with you.

How much does a website cost? +

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design complexity, functionality, and content needed. Share your requirements and we'll prepare a tailored quote before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile? +

Yes. Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop are part of the intended build approach, with content and navigation adjusted for smaller screens.

Can you help with domain and hosting? +

Yes, we can discuss domain, hosting, and deployment as part of the project. These accounts and any third-party charges should be agreed clearly before launch.

Do you provide website maintenance? +

Maintenance or future updates can be discussed as a separate arrangement, depending on the website and the support you need.
